Help-Desk Ticket Routing for a City Library System

SituationSet by your guide, shared by the team

The library’s help desk received about 1,400 tickets a month by email, and one in three went to the wrong team first.

ActionYours alone

Built the labeling set from six months of closed tickets. Let a model suggest the route, and wrote the rule for when a ticket goes to a person instead.

OutcomeAs you report it

Misrouted tickets fell from 1 in 3 to 1 in 12 over a four-week pilot.
