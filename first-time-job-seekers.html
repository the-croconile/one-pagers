For first-time job seekers

No experience doesn’t mean no evidence.

Your capstone, internship and hackathon work already happened. Capture it as a WorkCard, and the people who were there add their own accounts.

A transcript says you passed. A WorkCard shows what you built, and who was there.

Where it breaks

Your first job shouldn’t be your first evidence.

Grades don’t show the work.

A transcript never says what you built, or who you built it with.

Group work hides your part.

One grade, split five ways. Nobody can tell which calls were yours.

Entry-level roles ask for experience.

The projects you’ve already done rarely count as any.

WorkCard™ExampleHarlow State University · Capstone, Industrial Engineering · 2026

Shift Scheduler for Campus Dining

Situation

Weekly schedules for 120 student staff were built by hand, and roughly 40 shifts a week were swapped at the last minute.

Action

Built an availability intake and a constraint-based scheduler, then ran a two-week pilot with the dining managers.

Outcome

Last-minute swaps fell from about 40 a week to 11 across 120 student staff.

Why it holds

You write your account. You can’t write theirs. Each account sits on its author’s own record, never merged into yours.
