WorkCard™ExampleKestrel Mutual · Director, Customer Operations · 2025

One Queue for Three Regional Desks

Situation

Three regional desks ran separate queues and tools. The same case was often routed up to four times before anyone owned it.

Action

Merged the desks into one queue with shared triage rules. Decided which calls to push down to team leads. Let AI draft the routing, and kept people on every escalation.

Outcome

Handoffs per case fell from 3.2 to 1.4. First-contact resolution rose from 58% to 71%.
