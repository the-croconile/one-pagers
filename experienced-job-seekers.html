WorkCard™ExampleMeridian Home Goods · Senior Logistics Manager · 2025

Lane-Level Freight Visibility

Situation

Freight spend was reported as one monthly number. Nobody could see which of the 14 lanes were driving six straight quarters of increases.

Action

Built a lane-level cost and on-time model from carrier invoices, ran a competitive rebid, and kept two incumbents where service data justified it.

Outcome

Cost per shipment fell 11% year over year, with on-time held at 96%.

Why it holds
