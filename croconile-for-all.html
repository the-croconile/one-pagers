A work intelligence platform for the AI era

Your best work shouldn’t disappear when the project ends.

Capture your work as WorkCards. The people who were there add their own perspectives, creating a record no one person could write alone. Over time, every record compounds, revealing patterns in problems solved, capabilities demonstrated, and outcomes achieved across people, roles, companies, and domains.

AI-polished narratives are everywhere. Credible work signals are scarce.

Why

Three problems. One cause: real work becomes invisible.

01 · Memory problem

The project ends. What you did starts to fade.

Teams move on. Context goes with them.

02 · Signal problem

AI can polish the story. It can’t show the work.

When every resume reads well, none of them stands out.

03 · Capability problem

Human + AI work blurs who did what.

Titles can’t show the judgment behind the outcome.
