WorkCard™ExampleArdent Foods · Senior Financial Analyst · 2026

Intercompany Close Exceptions Queue

Situation

Month-end close took nine working days, most of it spent chasing 1,200 unmatched intercompany entries by hand.

Action

Built a matching-rules layer and an exceptions queue owned by each entity. Directed an AI model to draft match suggestions, and set the rule that nothing posts automatically.

Outcome

Close shortened from nine working days to five across 14 entities.
